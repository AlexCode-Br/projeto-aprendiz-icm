# Imagem do login

- Arquivo da versão anterior, substituído no login pela foto enviada pelo usuário: `src/assets/login-musicos.jpg`.
- Criado com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens (não CLI/API).
- Pessoas adultas fictícias. A referência do usuário orientou somente a roupa e o lenço vermelho.
- Original PNG preservado fora do repositório. JPEG de 191.883 bytes para carregamento rápido, sem alterar a composição.

Both seen from the side / three-quarter profile, focused on their instruments, never looking at the camera. Keep both musicians and recognizable, anatomically correct instruments fully readable in a narrow portrait crop, waist-up composition with comfortable margins. Warm softly blurred indoor music rehearsal setting, natural soft window lighting, realistic skin and fabric texture, refined authentic editorial photography. Red, neutral gray and warm white color accents. No lettering, logos, overlays, watermark, certificate, extra people or UI. Leave a calm area near the top for the existing website logo to be placed by code. The people must be fictional adults, not a reproduction of the person in the user's clothing reference.
